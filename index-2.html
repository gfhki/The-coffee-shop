Our Craft

From soil to cup, nothing is rushed

Three decisions shape every bag we roast — where the beans come from, how we treat the fire, and how we put water and coffee together. We take all three seriously.

01

Ethically Sourced Beans

We work directly with growers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra, paying well above commodity price and visiting the farms we buy from. Every cherry is hand-picked at peak ripeness — no stripping the branch, no shortcuts.

  • Direct-trade relationships with 12 farming families
  • Traceable lot numbers on every bag
  • Shade-grown, biodiversity-friendly farms
02

Patient, Small-Batch Roasting

Our roaster tends drums of no more than 12kg at a time, tracking bean temperature in five-second intervals. Every profile is built around the bean in front of them, not a recipe from last month.

  • 12kg max batch size for even heat transfer
  • Roasted in-house, never more than 10 days before sale
  • Cupped and scored before every batch ships
03

Artisan Brewing Techniques

Behind the counter, every method — pour-over, espresso, cold brew — is dialed in by weight, not guesswork. Our baristas train for six weeks before they pull a shot for a customer.

  • Scales and timers on every station
  • Water filtered and mineral-balanced for extraction
  • House blends recalibrated every new harvest
